# LectureLens 📚

**Turning forgotten lecture photos into structured, searchable study notes.**

## 💡 The Idea

Students take hundreds of photos during lectures: whiteboards, slides, handwritten notes, equations, diagrams.

Most of these photos end up forgotten in their camera rolls, rarely opened again.

LectureLens aims to turn this unstructured collection of images into useful study material.

The idea is simple: upload lecture photos and let AI extract, organize, and structure the information into readable notes.

## 🎯 The Problem

- Lecture photos are scattered across phone galleries.
- Important information is difficult to retrieve later.
- Handwritten content, equations, and diagrams are not easily searchable.
- Students often spend more time organizing notes than actually studying them.

## ✨ Proposed Features

- **OCR and extraction:** Extract text, equations, and relevant content from lecture photos.
- **Structured notes:** Organize extracted information into sections and coherent notes.
- **Context preservation:** Keep related photos and information grouped by course, subject, or lecture.
- **Search:** Find information across previously uploaded lecture material.
- **Study assistance:** Eventually, generate summaries, explanations, and questions from the collected material.

## 🧠 The Vision

Not another note-taking app.

A tool that gives the information already sitting in students' camera rolls a second life.
